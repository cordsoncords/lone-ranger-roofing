Stand on a dark commercial roof in Temple on an August afternoon and you'll feel the heat through your boot soles. A black membrane or dark metal panel can top 150 degrees at the surface on a clear day, and that heat has to go somewhere. On most buildings along I-35 and around the medical district, a good share of it goes down through the deck and into the space your air conditioning is fighting to keep cool.

That's the whole case for cool roofing, and it's worth saying plainly because the sales pitch around it tends to get inflated. A reflective roof won't cool a building by itself. What it does is stop the roof from acting like a heater sitting on top of your ceiling. How much that's worth depends on your building, and that's the part Temple business owners should understand before spending a dollar.

## What Cool Roofing Actually Does

Cool roof materials reflect more sunlight and release absorbed heat faster than conventional dark roofs. The common options on commercial buildings are white or light-colored TPO membranes, reflective elastomeric coatings applied over an existing roof, and light-colored or specially coated metal panels. Each one lowers the roof's surface temperature, sometimes by 50 degrees or more on a hot afternoon.

A cooler roof surface means less heat moving into the building, so rooftop HVAC units spend fewer hours running at full load. The units themselves also sit in cooler air, and they work more efficiently when they aren't pulling in superheated air off a black roof.

## Where Energy Efficiency Gains Are Biggest in Temple

The buildings that benefit most are the ones where the roof deck sits close to the conditioned space. Warehouses and shops with open ceilings, metal buildings with thin insulation, and strip retail with a shallow cavity above the drop ceiling all feel the difference quickly. An office building with thick insulation and a deep ceiling cavity will still see a benefit, though a smaller one.

Operating hours matter too. A distribution space running air conditioning ten or twelve hours a day through a Central Texas summer has a lot more to gain than a building that sits mostly empty in the afternoons. We walk owners through those factors honestly, because the right answer for a busy shop floor and the right answer for a small church office aren't the same.

## Longer Roof Life Is the Benefit People Forget

The energy savings get the headlines, but durability may matter more over time. Heat is hard on roofing materials. Every day a membrane swings from a mild morning to a scorching afternoon, it expands and contracts, and that cycling is what loosens seams and cracks flashing over the years. A roof that runs 50 degrees cooler at peak goes through far less of that stress.

For [TPO roofing](/commercial-roofing/tpo-roofing), a white membrane is the standard for good reason. It holds up well to Temple's UV exposure and heat, and it's straightforward to repair when hail comes through. Reflective coatings can also extend the life of an aging roof that isn't ready for replacement, buying a few more years while an owner plans the budget.

## What to Watch For With Cool Roofs

Cool roofs lose some reflectivity as they get dirty. Dust, pollen, and debris settle on the surface, and in a dry, windy summer that happens faster than you'd expect. A cleaning during routine maintenance brings most of it back. Coatings also have to go over a sound roof. Applying one over a wet or failing substrate traps the problem instead of fixing it, and we've seen owners pay twice because someone skipped that check.

If your building has a [commercial metal roof](/commercial-roofing/commercial-metal-roofing), factory-applied reflective finishes hold their color and reflectivity for decades, which makes metal a strong option for owners who would rather not think about recoating.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are cool roofs worth it for commercial buildings in Temple?**
For most buildings with high summer cooling loads, yes. The savings are largest on warehouses, metal buildings, and spaces with little insulation between the roof and the conditioned area.

**Can a cool roof coating go over an existing commercial roof?**
Often, if the existing roof is dry and structurally sound. We check for trapped moisture and failing seams first, since coating over those problems hides them.

**Do cool roofs hold up to hail in Central Texas?**
White TPO and coated metal handle hail about as well as their darker versions. If a storm does damage the roof, we help owners document it properly for the insurance claim.
