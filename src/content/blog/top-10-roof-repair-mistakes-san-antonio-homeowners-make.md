San Antonio homeowners deal with a rough combination of weather: hail that rolls through fast, heat that bakes shingles for months at a time, and enough humidity to make small leaks turn into mold problems before anyone notices. That mix means roof repair decisions come up often here, sometimes on a tight timeline right after a storm.

Most of the repair problems we get called out to fix in San Antonio didn't start with a bad shingle or a rare defect. They started with a decision made earlier in the process, often before we ever got involved. Here are the ten mistakes that show up most, in the order they tend to happen.

## Mistakes That Happen Before the Repair Even Starts

**1. Waiting too long to call after noticing damage.** A missing shingle or a small leak rarely stays small. Water finds its way into decking and insulation the longer it sits, turning a same-day fix into a bigger job.

**2. Hiring the first crew that knocks on the door.** After a San Antonio storm, out-of-town crews canvas neighborhoods offering fast, cheap repairs. Some do fine work. Many are gone before a warranty claim would ever matter.

**3. Skipping the inspection and going straight to a quote.** A [roof inspection](/residential-roofing/roof-inspection) tells us what's actually damaged versus what looks fine from the ground. Without it, repairs get scoped based on guesswork.

**4. Not documenting the damage before repairs begin.** Photos taken right after a storm, before any work starts, are what make an insurance claim move smoothly. Homeowners who repair first and file later often run into disputes about when the damage happened.

## Mistakes That Happen During the Repair

**5. Approving a repair without understanding what it covers.** A quote that lists nothing more than "patch damaged area" leaves too much open to interpretation. A clear scope should specify which shingles, which flashing, and whether the decking underneath gets checked.

**6. Assuming all repairs get inspected for hidden damage.** Hail damage in particular can be scattered across a roof in spots that aren't visible without getting up there. A repair limited to the one visible leak can leave other damaged areas untouched.

**7. Mixing repair materials that don't match the existing roof.** A patch done with the wrong shingle color, weight, or age can stand out and, in some cases, affect how an adjuster views the roof's condition on a future claim.

**8. Not asking whether the flashing gets addressed.** Most roof leaks in San Antonio trace back to flashing around chimneys, vents, and valleys, not the shingles themselves. A repair that swaps shingles but leaves old flashing in place often leaks again within a season.

## Mistakes That Happen After the Repair Is Done

**9. Assuming the repair is the end of it.** A repair addresses the damage that's there. It doesn't replace the value of a routine [roof tune-up](/residential-roofing/roof-tune-up) that catches new wear before it becomes another repair call.

**10. Losing track of the warranty terms.** Warranties that cover materials but not labor leave homeowners exposed if the repair itself was the problem. We warranty our labor the same way we warranty the materials, because that's usually where repair failures actually come from.

## FAQ

**How much does a typical roof repair cost in San Antonio?**
It depends on the extent of damage and whether flashing or decking needs attention along with the shingles. A roof inspection gives us a real number instead of a rough guess.

**Can a repair cover part of my roof, or does storm damage mean full replacement?**
Storm damage is often repairable if it's limited to a specific area. Full replacement becomes the better move when the roof's overall age or condition means more damage is likely regardless of one repair.

**How do I know if a repair crew is going to be around if something goes wrong?**
Ask directly how long they've operated in San Antonio and what their warranty actually covers. A crew with local roots and a written labor warranty has a reason to stand behind the work.

**Should I file an insurance claim before or after getting a repair estimate?**
Get a professional inspection and document the damage first. That record supports the claim, and we can walk through the [storm damage repair](/residential-roofing/storm-damage-repair) process alongside the adjuster once the claim is open.
